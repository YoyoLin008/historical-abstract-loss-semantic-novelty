The authoritative project was at `dd11aa3146744e7bd467d1d717ecd557b126de6c` when this draft began. **Execution requires a resolved originality gate, accepted data-quality audit, frozen inputs/seeds/code, and a separate freeze manifest recording the actual Git commit and timestamp.** That future commit is not invented here.

## Question and defensible target

Can a predefined aggregate comparison remain within a small operational tolerance while the identity of papers selected by a semantic-novelty ranking changes beyond a separately defined selection tolerance? Does structured historical abstract missingness add instability beyond opportunity-matched random missingness?

The reference is the fully observed **selected ACL/EMNLP collection**, not complete science or true scientific novelty. The target distribution is repeated mask assignments generated by the specified mechanisms, conditional on the frozen collection, encoder and focal cohort. It is not variation over independently sampled fields, historical databases or researchers. The protocol does not assume that the pilot pattern will recur.

## Fixed interventions and controls

Rates are 10%, 25% and 50%; zero masking is a deterministic baseline. All three are synthetic scenarios; 50% is a stress test. Do not label 10% or 25% empirically realistic without external evidence. Exact count is `floor(n_history × rate + 0.5)`.

Keep the pilot's outcome-independent mechanisms unchanged: uniform random masks; source preference ACL with weight 8 versus 1; lexical-proxy stratum 0 (translation) with weight 8 versus 1. Fixed title-keyword assignments are not validated scientific topics. Weighted sampling uses independent exponential clocks without replacement. Do not choose a different preferred source/stratum based on effect size.

For each independently drawn structured mask generate exactly one independently seeded matched control:

|Structured mask|Random-control cells|Exactly preserved|Permitted contrast interpretation|
|---|---|---|---|
|Source-preferred|Historical year|Total missing count and each year's missing count|Source location plus any associated within-year content differences, beyond historical age composition|
|Lexical-proxy-preferred|Historical year × source|Total count and every year/source cell's missing count|Lexical-proxy location and associated within-cell content differences, beyond historical year **and source** composition|

Within each cell, choose uniformly without replacement the structured mask's cell count. Do not also match source for the source intervention: that would remove its intended contrast. Do not match the lexical stratum in its own control: that would remove its intended contrast. Report cells with zero/full masking and the number of cells with nondegenerate control randomization. For a single 2021 focal cohort, all declared historical records are eligible for all focal papers; temporal matching also fixes eligible-history opportunity exactly. No control claims to reproduce actual database omissions or match title-neighborhood exposure.

The pilot used year-only controls for both mechanisms. Adding source matching to the confirmatory lexical-proxy control is an explicit prospective protocol change; do not retrospectively rename the pilot control.

## Exactly three primary outcomes

Let `b_i` be full-history novelty, `z_im` the score under mask m, and `s0 = SD_population(b_i)` over **all frozen eligible focal papers**, with `ddof=0`. A zero `s0` blocks standardized confirmation rather than triggering a new denominator. ACL and EMNLP are groups A and B.

1. **Aggregate distortion G:** `G_m = abs[(mean_A z_m − mean_B z_m) − (mean_A b − mean_B b)] / s0`. Compute the absolute value for each mask, then average over masks. Do not use the absolute value of the mean signed distortion.
2. **Top-decile replacement T:** let `k = ceil(0.10 × n_focal)` and let S0 and Sm contain exactly k papers sorted by descending score with lexical record ID breaking exact score ties. `T_m = |S0 \ Sm| / k`. Report expected numbers of baseline selections lost as well as fractions, and always state k. This is replacement among the selected decile, not a percentage of all papers.
3. **Mean normalized rank displacement Q:** `Q_m = mean_i |r_im − r_i0| / (n_focal − 1)`. Ranks use average ranks for exact score ties. Sorting IDs for top-k membership must not manufacture rank movement among equal-score papers. This endpoint summarizes rank displacement over the whole frozen cohort.

Use the single within-year ACL-minus-EMNLP contrast. Group sizes are fixed; no resampling or weighting is introduced merely to equalize source sizes. Undefined scores do not silently change the cohort. Empty histories are a design failure for the main arm; empty complete-case histories yield explicitly undefined ablation outcomes and exclusion counts.

Secondary descriptive outcomes: signed group distortion and original contrast, score drift, the rank-movement distribution, mask-distribution quantiles, source-specific selection gains/losses, and optional 5%/20% selection overlaps. The existing failed coverage diagnostic remains visible and secondary. No model or feature search uses the already-inspected pilot topic holdout.

## Practical tolerances and joint logic

Primary tolerances are `tau_G = 0.10` baseline SD, `tau_T = 0.05` of baseline top-decile selections, and `tau_Q = 0.01` of the cohort's rank range. These are transparent **study-design operating choices**, not disciplinary standards, validated policy losses, or universal definitions of important scientific change.

- A tenth of the baseline score SD defines a small aggregate perturbation relative to observed between-paper variation. It is not proof that the ACL-minus-EMNLP comparison has no bias. When the original group contrast is near zero, ratios and sign reversals are particularly misleading; report the original standardized contrast beside the distortion.
- Five percent replacement operationalizes retaining at least 95% of the baseline selection. With k selected papers it corresponds to 0.05k expected replacements, not five percent of the whole corpus. Discreteness in individual masks is retained; inference concerns the mean over masks. There is no externally validated hiring/funding decision attached to this threshold.
- One percent of the rank range expresses an average movement of approximately one percentile. It is an independent interpretive yardstick. It must not be numerically compared with 0.1 SD or 5% replacement.

For each mechanism/rate, let simultaneous intervals be `[L_G,U_G]`, `[L_T,U_T]`, and `[L_Q,U_Q]`. The predeclared classifications are:

|Rule|Permitted statement|
|---|---|
|`U_G < 0.10`|Mean aggregate distortion is bounded below the selected tolerance in this condition|
|`L_G > 0.10`|Mean aggregate distortion exceeds that tolerance|
|Otherwise|Aggregate stability is unresolved at that tolerance|
|`U_G < 0.10` **and** `L_T > 0.05`|Tolerance-defined aggregate stability coexists with mean top-decile selection instability|
|The preceding joint rule **and** `L_Q > 0.01`|The same condition additionally exceeds the prespecified whole-cohort mean rank-movement tolerance|
|`U_T < 0.05` or `U_Q < 0.01`|The corresponding mean instability is bounded below its own tolerance|

Equality or an interval touching a threshold is inconclusive. This is an intersection of outcome-specific claims, not an arbitrary ratio of incompatible units. Passing the selection rule alone does not establish broad rank disorder or scientifically important changes in papers' originality. Report all nine conditions; do not select the most dramatic one. A range statement is permitted only for tested conditions that individually satisfy the rule. No monotonic interpolation between 10%, 25% and 50% is assumed.

Prespecified sensitivity uses the same simultaneous intervals at `tau_G ∈ {0.05,0.10,0.15}`, `tau_T ∈ {0.025,0.05,0.10}`, and `tau_Q ∈ {0.005,0.01,0.02}`. Present a compact classification grid. These are not additional fitted models or newly selected endpoints. Never replace the primary thresholds after seeing confirmation. Claims of “most masks” require the separately reported empirical mask distribution; confidence intervals on means do not establish that statement.

## Mandatory cutoff-margin and movement analysis

Before interpreting top-decile loss, construct the following from the complete-cohort baseline and saved mask scores; these are evaluation quantities, not deployment-time predictors:

1. Report `b_(k)`, `b_(k+1)`, their gap, and the cutoff midpoint `c0 = (b_(k)+b_(k+1))/2`. Report raw and `s0`-standardized gaps. If the gap is zero, disclose the whole tied block and how many members are selected by ID. Do not portray swaps within that block as substantive score changes.
2. Give each paper the signed baseline score margin `(b_i−c0)/s0`. Use fixed signed bins defined by absolute edges `{0,0.01,0.025,0.05,0.10,infinity}`. Bins are left-closed/right-open; exact zero enters [0,0.01). Report all bin sizes, keeping empty bins visible. For baseline-selected papers report retention probability across masks; for initially unselected papers report entry probability. Plot bin-average probabilities with mask-cluster uncertainty only as descriptive support; do not attach independent-paper standard errors.
3. Define the cutoff rank neighborhood as the baseline 9th–11th percentiles of descending novelty rank: use `(r−0.5)/n` for descending ordinal rank r=1,...,n and the inclusive interval [0.09,0.11], expanded to include all exact-score ties crossing either edge. For each mask report the fraction of original k selections lost inside and outside this fixed band, both using k as denominator, plus the conditional loss fraction among selected papers outside the band with that denominator stated separately.
4. For baseline-selected papers, report far-margin losses `F_m(delta) = count{i in S0\Sm: (b_i−c0)/s0 > delta} / k` at `delta ∈ {0.01,0.025,0.05,0.10}`. Also report their counts. A high T with negligible F and narrow rank displacement supports **boundary sensitivity**, not broad measurement failure.
5. Summarize the distribution of absolute normalized rank movement: median, 90th percentile, and fraction of papers moving more than five percentile points, computed per mask and then summarized over masks. Include Q and source-specific selection movements. Do not equate a mean above 0.01 with every paper moving substantially.

The principal coexistence claim is the joint G/T rule. Qualify it as boundary-concentrated when loss is concentrated near the cutoff and Q does not clearly exceed its tolerance. A stronger substantive interpretation requires the cutoff margins and movement distribution to show more than tiny exchanges among nearly tied papers; no new post-hoc threshold or encoder may be selected to rescue that interpretation. The independent collection is the next validation step if scientific value remains adequate.

## Replications and simultaneous inference

Fix **200 independent mask assignments per rate/mechanism**, hence 1,800 base masks plus 1,200 matched controls. Each matched structured/control pair is one intervention unit. Use an explicit new seed manifest, master seed `3216741901` and namespace `acl-2021-confirmation-v1`; store all seed vectors before outcome execution and keep pilot seed `20261004` excluded. No adaptive replicate increases for precision or significance after inspection. A runtime limitation permits pause/resume, not changing the inferential sample size.

One predeclared family contains 45 mask-distribution means:

- 27 main mixed-representation means: G, T and Q × 3 mechanisms × 3 rates.
- 18 paired excess means: structured minus its matched control for G, T and Q × 2 structured mechanisms × 3 rates.

For each mean use a two-sided Bonferroni-adjusted Student interval `mean ± t_(199, 1−0.05/(2×45)) × sample_SD/sqrt(200)`. The critical value is approximately **3.30905952115116**; conservatively rounding upward to 3.309060 is acceptable. The mean intervals may be intersected with known mathematical bounds, but the original untruncated intervals must remain machine-readable. This is a transparent **approximate** familywise procedure: Student intervals are exact for normal observations; bounded/skewed mask outcomes rely on the large-sample mean approximation at n=200. Bonferroni does not require independence between endpoints. Do not claim exact finite-sample coverage from the adjustment alone. References: [NIST mean intervals](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda352.htm) and [NIST Bonferroni method](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc473.htm), checked 2026-10-04.

Report mask SD and 5th/50th/95th percentiles separately from Monte Carlo SE and the simultaneous mean interval. Those answer different questions. A 95% Monte Carlo interval on an average is not a 95% prediction interval for an individual missing-history realization. Do not bootstrap focal papers as iid interventions. A predeclared whole-mask bootstrap may be a numerical sensitivity check only; it must preserve the complete outcome vector and structured/control pairing and may not replace a less favorable primary interval after inspection.

Also report the **descriptive joint-event frequency** `p_hat = mean_m I(G_m < 0.10 and T_m > 0.05)` in each condition, with its pointwise 95% Wilson binomial interval using n=200 independent complete masks. Keep this secondary interval explicitly outside the 45-mean simultaneous family; do not use it to make a multiplicity-controlled claim that coexistence occurs in “most masks.” This probability asks whether both events occur under the same mask; the primary mean-level joint rule concerns two expectations and does not imply that per-mask statement. Neither rule establishes that every paper is stable. Record strict inequalities and keep mask IDs for exact reproduction.

For structured effects define each replicate's differences `G_struct−G_control`, `T_struct−T_control`, `Q_struct−Q_control` first, then calculate mean/SD/SE. An interval excluding zero supports a directional difference in this finite-corpus mechanism, not an externally meaningful magnitude by itself. Do not claim no structure effect from non-rejection. The G/T/Q practical tolerances concern total instability, not automatically equivalent tolerances for paired excess.

## Scientific interpretation and stop conditions

The strongest objection is that averages cancel while a thresholded ranking amplifies tiny perturbations—an elementary fact of aggregation and selection, not a new discovery. The title-only/full contrast can shift near-identical scores, and a percentile cutoff forces exactly k selections even when those papers are nearly tied. Arbitrary tolerances can make “coexistence” look consequential when it is operationally harmless. Opportunity matching and more mask replications do not by themselves make that observation original.

The response must be empirical and restrained: locate whether a practically specified aggregate reporting rule can conceal selection sensitivity under a credible, explicitly bounded metadata intervention; quantify the effect, cutoff margins and matched-control excess; and show a useful coverage/stability boundary that survives untouched-cohort and, if warranted, independent-collection validation. If all replacements are tiny near-cutoff exchanges, Q remains small, structure adds little, and no actionable evaluation recommendation survives the closest literature, label the outcome a narrow robustness/threshold-sensitivity result and reassess publication value. Do not intensify masking, alter the preferred topic or tune a new diagnostic to manufacture a contribution.

The originality gate can stop the study before execution. After confirmation, a separate post-confirmation gate evaluates scientific value, precision, external validation needs and resource feasibility. No public release or submission is authorized by this protocol.
