# Third-party boundaries

No third-party library source, model weights, model card, publisher manuscript class, fonts, abstracts, literature PDFs or raw API responses are bundled. Dependency names/version pins identify independently acquired packages and do not relicense them. SPECTER and SPECTER2 are independently acquired models, with each source's terms applying. Source documents are accessed from their original providers, and provider terms remain applicable.

The original Python research implementation and new release tooling are attributed to Yunya Lin with substantial disclosed AI assistance. Synthetic fixture strings are test inputs, not acquired abstracts. Identifier projections contain bibliographic identifiers and source/year labels only. Numeric outputs and identifier/configuration/documentation collections are the author's own materials licensed under CC BY 4.0 to the extent the author can grant rights. Original Python code/tests use MIT. LICENSE and manifests/license_scope.csv define the exact split; bare facts and public-domain elements acquire no new exclusive rights.

The title-token lexical bucket is an original deterministic numerical assignment; no titles are distributed. Do not interpret it as a validated subject classification.

The author-approved grants do not cover private manuscripts or old Git history, and do not imply UIUC ownership or endorsement. Original scope wording in the explicitly named new licensing-policy files uses CC BY 4.0; standard license terms remain unmodified.
